WordKeeper Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

What data we collect

WordKeeper stores the words you save and the URLs of pages where you saved them. This data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage sync API.

Gmail access

WordKeeper requests permission to send email via your Gmail account solely to email your saved word list to yourself. We do not read, store, or share your emails.

Data sharing

WordKeeper does not send your data to any external servers. All data stays in your browser and your Gmail account.
